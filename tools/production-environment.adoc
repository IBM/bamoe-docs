The production environment focuses on operational efficiency and scalability. The Management Console simplifies administration and monitoring of deployed automation services, providing visibility and control. Business Services deliver the runtime engines for decision-making, rule execution, and workflow orchestration, ensuring that automation solutions perform consistently in live environments.
