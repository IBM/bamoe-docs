= Upgrading from 8.0.x
include::../partials/attributes.adoc[]
include::../styles/images.adoc[]

{PRODUCT_SHORT} version {VERSION} introduces a modern, cloud-native architecture based on the Kogito runtime. This shift brings significant changes in how processes, tasks, persistence, and user management are handled.

This section is designed to support teams upgrading from {PRODUCT_SHORT} version {VERSION_80X} to {PRODUCT_SHORT} version {VERSION}. It is structured to reflect the typical upgrade journey, from understanding key differences to transforming projects and assets. It defines a structured approach to the migration process, covering everything from environment setup and project upgrades to operational and performance considerations.

All roles in your team who are involved in the upgrade, i.e., architects, developers and business analysts will benefit from reading it. 

To get the most out of this guide, we suggest the sections in the Upgrade Guide in following order:

. *Start with the xref:02-key-differences.adoc[Key Differences] section*
+
Understand the architectural and conceptual shift between version {VERSION_80X} and version {VERSION}. This will help you frame the rest of the upgrade process.

. *Review the xref:04-development-environment-comparison.adoc[Development Environment] and upgrade your project using xref:05-00-upgrading-projects.adoc[Project Upgrade Strategies]*
+
These sections provide step-by-step guidance to upgrade your client-server or standalone projects. Configuration examples document the main project structure differences and demonstrate how to upgrade your assets.

. *Dive into xref:06-production-environment-comparison.adoc[Production Environment] Comparisons*
+
This section provides side-by-side insights into how the production environment has evolved in {PRODUCT_SHORT} version {VERSION_930}.

. *Understand xref:07-live-data-considerations.adoc[Live Data], xref:08-operation-considerations.adoc[Operational] and xref:09-performance-considerations.adoc[Performance] Considerations*
+
These sections help you plan for scaling, persistence, monitoring, and long-running processes.

. *Review the xref:11-not-supported.adoc[Not supported] features and xref:13-faq.adoc[FAQ] sections*
+
Learn about unsupported features and practical tips based on the most commonly asked questions.
