== Navigating this document

The informaiton is designed to support teams upgrading from BAMOE v8 to BAMOE v9, whether you are a architect, developer or business analyst. It is structured to reflect the typical upgrade journey, from understanding key differences to transforming projects and assets.

=== Recommended flow of reading

To get the most out of this guide, we suggest the following order:

    . Start with the Introduction and Key Differences.

    Understand the architectural and conceptual shifts between v8 and v9. This will help you frame the rest of the upgrade process.

    . Review the Development Environment and Production Environment Comparisons.

    These sections provide side-by-side insights into how tools, workflows, and runtime behavior have changed.

    . Dive into Project Upgrade Strategies.

    Whether you are upgrading client-server or standalone projects, these sections provide step-by-step guidance and configuration examples going through project structure differences and how to upgrade your assets.

    . Understand Live Data migration, Operational and Performance Considerations.

    These sections help you plan for scaling, persistence, monitoring, and long-running processes.

    . Check the Miscellaneous notes, Known Limitations and Not supported features.

    Be aware of unsupported features and practical tips that do not fit into other categories.

=== Document Structure

Each section includes:

    * Comparative insights between v8 and v9
    * Step-by-step instructions for common upgrade tasks
    * Code snippets and configuration examples
    * References to official documentation and community resources
