This document provides a comprehensive guide for migrating from Business Automation Manager Open Edition (BAMOE) v8 to BAMOE v9. As BAMOE evolves, version 9 introduces a modern, cloud-native architecture based on the Kogito runtime, offering improved scalability, flexibility, and integration capabilities. However, this shift also brings significant changes in how processes, tasks, persistence, and user management are handled.

To support a smooth transition, the document outlines a structured approach to the migration process, covering everything from environment setup and project upgrades to operational and performance considerations. It is intended for developers, architects, and administrators who are familiar with BAMOE v8 and are looking to transition to the new platform with confidence.
