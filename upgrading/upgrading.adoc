= Upgrading from 8.0.x

include::../partials/attributes.adoc[]


You can upgrade your existing business automation applications and platforms to the most recent version of {PRODUCT_LONG}. The supported upgrade process is from version {VERSION_80} to the latest version (9.2.1). Depending on the complexity of your installation, you might want IBM(R) Services to participate in your project. If so, contact your IBM service representative.

If you are in version {VERSION_80} and want to upgrade directly to version 9.2.x, follow this link: 
https://ibmdocs-test.dcs.ibm.com/docs/en/ibamoe/9.2.x?topic=upgrading-from-80x[Upgrading].
