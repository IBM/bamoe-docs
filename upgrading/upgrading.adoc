= Upgrading from 8.0.x

include::../partials/attributes.adoc[]

If you are on version {VERSION_80} you can now upgrade directly to the most recent version 9.2.1, see 
https://ibmdocs-test.dcs.ibm.com/docs/en/ibamoe/9.2.x?topic=upgrading[BAMOE 9.2.x → Upgrading from 8.0.x]. You will not need to upgrade progressively to intermediate versions as is usually advised. 

Depending on the complexity of your installation, you might want IBM Consulting to participate in your project. If so, contact your IBM representative.
